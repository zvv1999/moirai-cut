# Shared Loom

The visual system begins with a single visible timeline, not a field of magical
possibility. It is the shared surface where intent, Agent action, direct
manipulation and preview meet. Every composition should make this continuity
legible before any copy is read: one project, one state, one loop. The craft is
quiet and exact, with expert alignment, measured intervals and deliberate
negative space.

Two threads cross without merging into an anonymous automation. One represents
human judgment; the other represents the Agent's ability to inspect and
propose. They exchange position around the timeline because both participants
remain active throughout the edit. There is no handoff diagram and no heroic AI
centerpiece. The work should feel painstakingly constructed by a designer who
understands that collaboration is a repeated motion, not a one-click event.

Color behaves like edit state. Loom Black holds the working field, Living White
marks what can be read and controlled, and Thread Cyan identifies the current
shared decision. Supporting slate is reserved for inactive information. Any
violet or warm spectral accent exists only to separate depth in campaign art;
it never competes with the real product evidence or suggests mysticism.

Scale proves the product. The editor screenshot is the largest object because
the visible timeline, controls and preview are the claim. Small repeated frame
marks may lead into it, while one cyan thread can continue through the layout to
connect prompt, change and preview. Nothing is added merely to say “AI.” Expert
craftsmanship appears through restraint, hierarchy and the confidence to leave
large areas quiet.

Typography is sparse and operational. `MOIRAI / CUT`, `FROM BLACK BOX` and
`TO SHARED TIMELINE` may carry the launch field; the product loop and brand
promise are the only supporting lines. Each word must remain readable at final
distribution size. The finished material should feel cinematic but usable,
conceptual but evidenced, and refined through countless precise decisions.
