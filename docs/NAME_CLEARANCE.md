# Moirai Cut name-clearance status

**Status: public developer preview; conditional hold for formal brand launch.**
The existing public fork may use the name as a clearly labelled preview while
formal clearance is completed, but it must not claim exclusivity, registration
or completed legal clearance. This is an engineering search record, not legal
advice or a trademark opinion.

## Preliminary findings (2026-08-02)

Public exact-name searches found no repository named `moirai-cut` on GitHub and
no exact `moirai-cut` or `moiraicut` package on npm or PyPI. Those results are a
dated snapshot only: they can change, do not search every trademark or
common-law source and do not reserve a package, organization, domain, account
or mark. Domain and Rust package availability were not confirmed.

The base word **Moirai** already has public uses in adjacent areas. A
[no-code virtual serious-game authoring platform](https://doi.org/10.3390/virtualworlds1020009)
is named Moirai; [Devir publishes a board game named
Moirai](https://devirgames.com/moirai) whose product story also uses cards as a
loom; and a separate game named Moirai remains visible through its [Steam
community](https://steamcommunity.com/app/496920). These are not exact-name
video editors, but the overlap means the project must not claim that the word
or loom story is unique, available or legally cleared.

Greek mythology is ancient source material, but that does not make a modern
product name or logo automatically free of trademark, passing-off or consumer
confusion risk. The added word `Cut`, the original weave symbol and the precise
video-editing category improve distinctiveness; they do not replace a formal
search in the intended markets and classes.

## Required professional search

Before making the repository public under this brand, the owner or counsel must:

1. search exact and confusingly similar word and design marks in China, the
   United States, the European Union, the United Kingdom and other launch
   markets;
2. review relevant software, downloadable application, SaaS, media, education
   and entertainment goods/services, plus translations and common-law use;
3. review company names, app stores, package registries, domains and social
   handles;
4. document the owner, filing strategy and approved goods/services wording;
5. record a written go/no-go decision in the private release file.

Official starting points are the [CNIPA trademark
search](https://wcjs.sbj.cnipa.gov.cn/), [USPTO Trademark
Search](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search), [EUIPO Search
IP/TMview](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/en/search-ip) and [WIPO Global Brand
Database](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database). Absence from a
single database or search engine is not clearance.

## Repository rule

The current public fork may remain a developer preview while the search is in
progress. Do not rename the canonical remote, buy domains, publish packages,
announce a formal launch or claim registered or exclusive trademark rights
until the gate above is signed off. If clearance fails, replace the public name
and assets in one compatibility-preserving migration; protocol identifiers such
as `opencut://` should not be renamed as part of a cosmetic rebrand.
